01 / Without the ball

Defensive shape
& movement

23 sec

What to look for

  • Maintain a compact and organised defensive structure.
  • Move and shift together as a unit according to the ball’s position.
  • Keep the distances between our defensive lines tight and avoid leaving unnecessary gaps.
  • Everyone has a defensive responsibility, not just the back four.

Forwards & holding midfielders

  • When our two forwards defend well and our two holding midfielders hold their positions, it’s much harder for opponents to play through us.
  • If the forwards are tired, don’t rush into a press. Block the passing lanes to the opposition’s central holding midfielders first, and keep them covered when pressing.
  • The two holding midfielders must hold their positions. If one steps out to press, the other stays in front of the centre-backs to protect the space. Don’t both press at once.
  • Stick to the team’s tactical plan and keep our shape stable as a unit.

02 / With the ball

Attacking
build-up

10 sec

What to look for

  • Build from the back with composure and good positioning.
  • Create passing options and support the player on the ball.
  • Use width and movement to create space and progress forward.
  • Move the ball as a team rather than relying on individual play.

The bigger picture

Our foundation
for the season.

These two principles — defending together and building up together — will be our baseline throughout the season.

The objective isn’t to make things complicated. It’s to establish a common understanding of how we position ourselves, move and support each other, both with and without the ball.

Once we consistently execute these basics, we can gradually develop more advanced tactics and variations as a team.

Let’s build our team identity from these fundamentals. 💪⚽
